Transcript Editor

Correct a Word transcript while you listen

Drop your Word transcript (.docx) and the audio recording anywhere on this page, or choose them:

  1. In Word for the web, add the transcript to your document (with speakers and timestamps) and download it as a .docx.
  2. Download the recording too: it's in the Transcribed Files folder of your OneDrive. The .docx only links to it.
  3. Edit here, then Export .docx. Your edits are autosaved in this browser and your files never leave your computer.
